# Plant City Panel Fix — Safety Checklist

Electrical panel warning-sign checklist for Plant City homes.

A quick companion for plantcitypanelfix.com. Check your electrical panel against common warning signs and know when to call a licensed electrician. Safety guidance only — never open a live panel yourself.

## Download

Installable packages are on the [Releases](https://github.com/maria876david-debug/plant-city-panel-fix-extension/releases) page — Microsoft Edge (.zip) and Firefox (.zip).

## Website

https://plantcitypanelfix.com

## Privacy

No data is collected. The extension runs locally in your browser; no account, no tracking, no remote requests.
