# 5 Signs Your Plant City Electrical Panel Is Overloaded

Panels rarely fail without warning. These are the five signs we tell Plant City homeowners to watch for.

One, breakers that trip again and again — especially when the air conditioner starts or two big appliances run together. Two, lights that dim or flicker when a large appliance kicks on. Three, a panel that feels warm to the touch, or a faint burning smell near it. Four, buzzing or crackling sounds from inside the panel. Five, a Federal Pacific Stab-Lok or Zinsco panel: both brands were common in homes built from the 1950s through the 1980s, and both have a documented history of breakers failing to trip — the one job a breaker must never fail at.

An isolated problem — one failed breaker with a clean bus bar behind it — is usually a repair, typically $150–$500 in the Plant City area. A panel that is itself the problem, or a third repair on the same panel, is a replacement conversation: most residential replacements in the area fall between $1,800 and $3,500, with a firm written price before any work starts. And if the panel is healthy but you are out of capacity — an EV charger, a pool, a workshop — that is an upgrade, and it starts with a load calculation.
