# Repair or Replace? How to Tell What Your Electrical Panel Actually Needs

Not every panel problem means a new panel, and not every cheap repair is a bargain. The rule we publish at Plant City Panel Fix is simple, and we apply it whether it makes us more money or less.

Repair it when the problem is isolated: one failed breaker with a clean, solid bus bar behind it, one loose connection that needs proper torque, one circuit at fault. Repairs like these typically run $150–$500 in the Plant City area. Replace it when the panel itself is the problem: a pitted or heat-damaged bus bar, a Federal Pacific Stab-Lok or Zinsco brand, a fuse box blowing fuses monthly, or the third repair on the same panel — at that point the repairs cost more than the replacement would have.

And if the panel is healthy but you are out of capacity — an EV charger, a pool, a workshop — that is neither repair nor replacement; it is an upgrade conversation that starts with a load calculation.
