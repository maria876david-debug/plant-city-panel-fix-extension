# Why Your Breaker Keeps Tripping When the AC Starts in Plant City

A breaker that trips every time the air conditioning starts is doing its job - it is telling you the circuit is overloaded. The usual causes are an air conditioner and another heavy appliance sharing one circuit, a breaker that has weakened after years of tripping, or a compressor drawing more current as it ages in the Florida heat. What not to do: keep resetting the breaker, or swap in a bigger breaker to "fix" it - that removes the protection and lets the wiring overheat. What to do: note which breaker trips and what else is on that circuit, then call a licensed electrician to check the load and the breaker itself. A tripping breaker is a warning, not an annoyance.
