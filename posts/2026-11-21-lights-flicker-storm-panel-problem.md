# Why Your Lights Flicker in a Plant City Storm - and When It Is a Panel Problem

Lights flicker in a Florida storm and most people blame the weather - and most of the time, the weather is the answer. But there is a difference between storm flicker and a panel problem, and it is worth knowing. If the lights dim briefly when the AC starts, that is normal: a big motor draws a surge of current. If the flicker happens on a calm day, in one room only, or along with a warm panel, a burning smell, or a breaker that keeps tripping, the problem is inside your house, not outside it. Loose connections, a worn main breaker, or a panel past its working life all show up as flicker first and as something worse later. The rule is simple: flicker that follows the storm is weather; flicker that follows no pattern is a call to a licensed electrician. Never ignore a panel that feels warm or smells hot.
