# When to Call an Electrician About Your Panel - and When to Stop Using It

Your electrical panel gives warnings before it fails. A panel that feels warm, a burning smell near the breakers, breakers that trip again and again, lights that flicker when an appliance starts, or a panel that buzzes are all reasons to call an electrician - not to wait and see.

Older Federal Pacific and Zinsco panels are a special case: both are known for breakers that can fail to trip, which is a fire risk, and many insurers now ask about them. Never open the panel yourself and never keep resetting a tripping breaker. Turn off the affected circuit if you can do so safely, and get a licensed electrician to look at it. A panel problem is one of the few home repairs that should never be a DIY job.
