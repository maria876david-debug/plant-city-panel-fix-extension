# Warm Outlets or a Burning Smell: What to Do Right Away

A warm outlet or a burning smell near a socket is not a wait-and-see problem - it is your wiring asking for help in the only language it has. Do this, in order: stop using that outlet immediately and unplug what is in it, by the plug, not the cord. If the smell is strong or you see scorch marks, switch the circuit off at the panel and leave it off. Do not keep testing the outlet to check if it still works, and do not cover the smell and carry on - heat at an outlet usually means a loose connection arcing or a wire overheating, and both get worse, not better. Then call a licensed electrician and describe exactly what you felt and smelled; that detail helps them find it fast. Electrical faults rarely fix themselves, and the ones that smell are already past the warning stage.
