# Old Wiring in a Florida Home: 5 Signs It Needs an Electrician's Look

Florida heat ages wiring faster than most homeowners realise, and an old panel hides the warning signs until something trips - or worse. Five signs your wiring needs an electrician's look: the panel feels warm to the touch, the lights dim when a big appliance starts, you smell something hot or burnt near the panel, the same breaker trips more than once, and the panel is a Federal Pacific or Zinsco model, which are known for failing to trip when they should. Any one of these is a reason to call a licensed electrician, not a reason to wait and see. Never keep resetting a tripping breaker, and never swap in a bigger breaker to stop the tripping - that removes the protection and lets the wiring overheat. A tripping breaker is a warning, not an annoyance.
